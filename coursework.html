Selected Coursework

These are some selected courses I've taken, or I'm taking.

MAT1011 Honours Calculus I Fall 2025
  • Institution: The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen
  • Instructor: Prof. PAN Xingbin
  • Description: This course and its continuation MAT1012 are designed for those students who are interested in mathematics, and who wish to build a solid foundation for more advanced courses. These courses are aimed to develop students’ abilities of analysing, problem-solving, mathematical thinking, reasoning and standardized expression. The course MAT1011 deals with the basic theory and skills of calculus of single variable. Topics include functions, limits and continuity, differentiation, integrability, integration and improper integrals.
MAT1012 Honours Calculus II Spring 2026
  • Institution: The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen
  • Instructor: Prof. PAN Xingbin
  • Description: MAT1012 teaches multi-variables calculus with depth. Topics include theory of real numbers, infinite sequences and series (of numbers and of functions), uniform convergence, power series, Taylor expansions, implicit function theorems, vectors and spaces, partial derivatives and differentiation, differentiability, integrability, double and iterated integrals, triple integrals, integrals depending on parameters, integration of vector fields, line and surface integrals, Green’s theorem, Stokes’ theorem，divergence theorem.
MAT2042 Honours Linear Algebra Spring 2026
  • Institution: The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen
  • Instructor: Prof. NI Weiming
  • Description: This course introduces the fundamental concepts and techniques of linear algebra, including: vectors, vector spaces, system of linear equations, matrices, determinants, linear transformations, eigen-theory, diagonalization, Jordan canonical form, matrix exponential, orthogonality, the Spectral Theorem.
CS229 Machine Learning Summer 2026
  • Institution: Leland Stanford Junior University
  • Instructors: Jehangir Amjad & Anand Avati
  • Description: This course provides a broad introduction to machine learning and statistical pattern recognition. Topics include: supervised learning (generative learning, parametric/non-parametric learning, neural networks); unsupervised learning (clustering, dimensionality reduction); learning theory (bias/variance tradeoffs, practical advice); reinforcement learning and adaptive control. The course will also discuss recent applications of machine learning, such as to robotic control, data mining, autonomous navigation, bioinformatics, speech recognition, and text and web data processing.
EE364a Convex Optimization I Summer 2026
  • Institution: Leland Stanford Junior University
  • Instructors: Logan Bell & Nikhil Devanathan
  • Description: Concentrates on recognizing and solving convex optimization problems that arise in applications. Convex sets, functions, and optimization problems. Basics of convex analysis. Least-squares, linear and quadratic programs, semidefinite programming, minimax, extremal volume, and other problems. Optimality conditions, duality theory, theorems of alternative, and applications. Interior-point methods. Applications to signal processing, statistics and machine learning, control and mechanical engineering, digital and analog circuit design, and finance.
MAT2001 Honours Ordinary Differential Equations Fall 2026
  • Institution: The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen
  • Instructor: Prof. NI Weiming
  • Description: This course teaches fundamental principles and methods of ordinary differential equations with rigorous theory and sophisticated techniques. It covers the topics of first order differential equations, second order linear equations, two point boundary-value problems, systems of linear equations, and qualitative theory of nonlinear differential equations, as well as their applications.
MAT2060 Honours Mathematical Analysis Fall 2026
  • Institution: The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen
  • Instructor: Prof. NI Weiming
  • Description: The contents and teaching methods of this course emphasize comprehensive understanding of the theory and methods of mathematical analysis, and cultivate students' ability of in-depth analysis, rigorous reasoning, and courage to innovate. Topics include the systems of real and complex numbers, point set topology in Euclidean spaces, metric spaces, Baire category theorem, differentiation and integration, implicit function theorem and its application, sequences and series of functions, space of continuous functions, Fourier series, Fourier transform and Laplace transform, vector field theory and applications, Stokes’ and Divergence Theorems.
MAT3040 Advanced Linear Algebra Fall 2026
  • Institution: The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen
  • Instructor: Prof. WONG Kayue
  • Description: The topics include vector spaces and subspaces, linear transformations and matrix representations, quotient and dual spaces, universal mapping property and tensor products, exterior algebra and determinants, eigenvalues and eigenvectors, primary decomposition theorem and Jordan normal form, finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces, self-adjoint operators, normal operators and the spectral theorem.
STA2001H Honours Probability and Statistics I Fall 2026
  • Institution: The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen
  • Instructor: Prof. ZONG Wei
  • Description: This course develops relevant probability models and theoretical concepts to describe such uncertainties and variability with motivating practical problems. Topics include basic concepts of probability theory, discrete distributions, continuous distributions, generating functions, multivariate distributions, sampling distributions and limit theorems.
