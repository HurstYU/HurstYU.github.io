Music

Music, for me, is like a storehouse of memories or a time machine. Playing an album or a song magically brings me back to times when I listened to it regularly. The following gallery displays those songs or albums that have such magic. They are arranged according to time. Note that the number of tracks in the “favourites” section does not reflect how much I love the album. The “favourites” are those which have constructed a hook in my memory.

I began listening to CUI Jian’s music intensively with his second album, Jiejue, whose passionate rhythms accompanied me through my struggle with the challenging honors courses in the second semester of my freshman year. I started to pay attention to the lyrics, rhythms, and arrangements of songs. What’s more, I was deeply moved by Cui’s reflections on the times and the nation. I was motivated by the spiritual power of Rock ‘n’ Roll that his music conveyed.
I was obsessed with Cui’s music, which decreased the frequency of my listening to Dou’s. However, Dou's work still greatly attracted me. The most impressive one for me in that period was his fourth album, Yuxu. Yuxu’s sense of calm and Zen-like tranquility neutralized Jiejue’s restlessness and rebellion, which lifted my spirits in a completely different way.

I got to know DOU Wei in the first semester of my last year in high school. I fell in love with his music and began to admire the album's cohesiveness. Dou’s first three albums focus on a person’s interior world. I was struggling to prepare for the Gaokao (China’s College Entrance Exam) during that period, when I lacked interaction with others and constantly doubted myself. My mind overlapped a lot with Dou’s three albums, and they were the source of my spirits during that time. Dou’s albums have been changing my taste in music. Also, I sometimes looped the song "Pledge" by Faye Wong because of Dou’s impressive Dizi (a traditional Chinese instrument) performance.
